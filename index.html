About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, specializing in AI-driven agricultural robotics. My research focuses on designing, developing, and deploying fully functional autonomous agricultural robots for real-world field environments.

My expertise lies in tracked mobile manipulators, RGB-D perception, deep learning-based vision systems, and real-time AI deployment on embedded platforms. I integrate CNNs and Vision Transformers with multimodal sensors (RGB-D cameras, IMU, GPS) to enable fruit detection, maturity estimation, plant disease classification, soil moisture assessment, and autonomous navigation in unstructured agricultural terrains.
