About

Where Mechanical Engineering Meets Artificial Intelligence

My research lives at a fascinating intersection — designing physical robots that can perceive the world through cameras, understand what they see using deep learning, and act autonomously in the challenging, unpredictable environment of agricultural fields.

I build tracked mobile manipulators equipped with RGB-D sensors, Vision Transformers, and real-time AI running on edge devices. These systems detect ripe tomatoes, identify plant diseases, assess soil moisture, navigate obstacles, and perform precision harvesting — all without human intervention.

The goal isn't just academic research. It's creating technology that works in real fields, helping farmers grow more food more sustainably.

Research Focus

What I Work On

Developing end-to-end autonomous systems that combine mechanical design, computer vision, and deep learning for precision agriculture.

🤖

Autonomous Mobile Manipulators

Designing tracked robots with 5-DOF manipulators capable of navigating rough terrain and performing precision tasks like fruit harvesting using depth sensing and inverse kinematics.

👁️

Vision Transformers for Agriculture

Developing hybrid CNN-ViT architectures for fruit maturity prediction, plant disease classification, and crop health monitoring with high accuracy under real-world conditions.

🎯

Real-time Object Detection

Implementing YOLOv8/v9 pipelines for fruit detection, ripeness assessment, and obstacle avoidance — optimized for edge deployment on NVIDIA Jetson platforms.

🧭

Autonomous Navigation

Building navigation systems using sensor fusion (RGB-D, IMU, GPS) with Dynamic Window Approach and Model Predictive Control for safe traversal in unstructured environments.

🌱

Plant Health Assessment

Creating self-attention based models for cashew leaf disease detection and soil moisture classification using spectral analysis and transfer learning techniques.

⚡

Edge AI Deployment

Deploying real-time deep learning inference on Jetson Orin Nano, achieving sub-50ms latency for perception tasks critical to autonomous field operations.
