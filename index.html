About

Where Mechanical Engineering Meets Artificial Intelligence

I completed my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, where I worked on intelligent robotic systems for agriculture, and I now teach Robotics and AI as an Assistant Professor at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru. My work sits between mechanical design, embedded computing and deep learning: building machines that can perceive, decide and act on their own in unstructured field conditions.

For my doctoral research I built a tracked mobile manipulator with a 5-DOF arm and developed the perception that drives it. This included a YOLOv8 with self-attention for fruit detection, a cascade into a Vision Transformer for ripeness staging, hybrid CNN-Transformer models for plant disease classification, and models for soil moisture assessment. Everything runs onboard an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, optimised through ONNX and TensorRT for real-time inference.

I care about work that leaves the lab. The robot was tested across more than 50 field trials, where it picked fruit successfully 92% of the time and navigated crop rows without collisions. That translation from model to machine, and from machine to classroom, is what I enjoy most.

Research Domains

Technical Focus Areas

Developing comprehensive autonomous systems through the integration of mechanical design, perception algorithms, and intelligent control for precision agriculture applications.

🤖

Autonomous Mobile Manipulators

Design and development of tracked robotic platforms with multi-DOF serial manipulators, incorporating kinematic modeling, workspace optimization, and depth-guided manipulation for selective harvesting operations.

👁️

Vision Transformer Architectures

Development of hybrid CNN-ViT frameworks leveraging self-attention mechanisms for agricultural perception tasks including fruit maturity assessment, disease classification, and crop health monitoring.

🎯

Real-time Detection Systems

Implementation of optimized YOLO-based detection pipelines for fruit localization, ripeness classification, and obstacle detection with sub-50ms inference latency on embedded platforms.

🧭

Autonomous Navigation

Multi-sensor fusion frameworks integrating RGB-D cameras, IMU, and GPS with path planning algorithms including Dynamic Window Approach and Model Predictive Control for field navigation.

🌱

Agricultural Perception

Self-attention enhanced models for plant disease detection and soil moisture classification using transfer learning and explainable AI techniques for robust field deployment.

⚡

Edge AI Deployment

Optimization and deployment of deep learning models on NVIDIA Jetson platforms, enabling real-time inference for perception-action loops critical to autonomous agricultural operations.
