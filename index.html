About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, specializing in AI-driven agricultural robotics and computer vision. My research focuses on the design, development, and real-world deployment of autonomous tracked mobile manipulators capable of operating reliably in unstructured agricultural environments.

Working at the intersection of mechanical system design, embedded intelligence, and deep learning, I build complete robotic systems that integrate RGB-D perception, Vision Transformers, CNN-based object detection, and real-time AI inference on NVIDIA Jetson platforms. My applications span fruit detection and maturity estimation, plant disease classification, soil moisture assessment, and autonomous navigation with obstacle avoidance.

My work emphasizes translational research outcomes—moving from laboratory development to robust on-field deployment through extensive field trials, performance validation, and iterative system optimization for precision farming and sustainable agriculture.

Key Research Projects

🤖

AI-Integrated Autonomous Agricultural Robot

Design and deployment of a tracked mobile manipulator for autonomous navigation, real-time robotic fruit picking using depth sensing and inverse kinematics, and comprehensive crop monitoring in unstructured agricultural fields.

🍅

Tomato Maturity & Ripeness Prediction

YOLOv8/v9 and Vision Transformer-based pipeline for multi-stage tomato maturity classification. Developed cascade models achieving high accuracy with field-level validation and real-time inference capability.

🍃

Cashew Leaf Disease Classification

Hybrid CNN–Vision Transformer framework for robust cashew leaf disease detection. Achieved high classification accuracy under limited data conditions using self-attention mechanisms and transfer learning.

🌱

Soil Moisture Classification

Comparative study of Vision Transformer and VGG16 models for precision agriculture soil moisture detection using spectral data and sensor fusion techniques.

🚜

Autonomous Navigation & Obstacle Avoidance

Real-time obstacle avoidance system for autonomous tracked robots integrating YOLOv8-based vision framework with Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) and MPC path planning for safe field traversal.

🔬

Plant Health Assessment System

Deep learning techniques for agricultural plant health assessment including disease detection using CNN and self-attention models, achieving high classification accuracy for automated plant health monitoring.
