Professional Summary

I am a PhD Candidate in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur working at the intersection of autonomous robotics, computer vision, and artificial intelligence for precision agriculture. My research focuses on designing and deploying tracked mobile manipulators capable of real‑time perception, decision‑making, and manipulation in unstructured agricultural environments.

My work integrates RGB‑D cameras, IMU, and GPS with deep learning models such as CNNs and Vision Transformers, enabling fruit detection, maturity estimation, plant disease classification, soil moisture assessment, and safe autonomous navigation. I emphasize real‑world deployment, extensive field trials, and system‑level validation.
