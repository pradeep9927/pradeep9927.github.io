Professional Summary

I am a PhD Candidate in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kharagpur with deep expertise in AI-driven agricultural robotics. My work bridges mechanical system design, embedded intelligence, and advanced computer vision to build autonomous robotic platforms capable of operating reliably in unstructured agricultural environments.

I specialize in tracked mobile manipulators, RGB-D perception, deep learning models (CNNs and Vision Transformers), and real-time AI deployment on embedded platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson. My research has resulted in multiple peer-reviewed publications, an Indian granted patent, and extensive field validation of autonomous agricultural robots.
